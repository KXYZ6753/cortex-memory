# p6: prompt shape at large n, with a placebo arm (round 6)

Prefix `p6`. Code: `explore2/variants/p6-shape.js` (shapes, gold-only arms, end-to-end proxy), tools `explore2/tools/p6-check.js` (offline stub check), `p6-lib.js`, `p6-stats.js` (paired det-vs-det statistics).

Question: does prompt *shape* (where the question and the rules sit relative to the emails) move e2b's reading beyond a content-free perturbation, at large n and behind det? Prior evidence: the sandwich (question before and after the emails) gave +3.2 (gold-only 88.6 → 91.5); o4 (question, emails, rules, question) and o12 (o4 + mailbox dedup) were +0.7/+1.3 and +0.9/+0.8 on S300-2/S300-1 (300 each, no det, no placebo); every instruction added to the prompt lost (gatesf, gatesm, o1).

## 1. Design (fixed before any run, Wed 7 Oct 20:00 ET)

**Mechanism.** Causal attention: email tokens are encoded knowing the question only if the question precedes them. gemma4 e2b also has sliding-window layers (`n_swa = 512`, i.md): in those layers an email token sees the leading question only if it lies within the previous 512 tokens. In the sandwich, the rules block (~65 tokens) sits between the leading question and the emails.

**Shapes** (same tokens, one generate call each):

| shape | order | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| sandwich (parent) | intro, Question, Rules, Emails, Question, Answer | gates / oracles prompt |
| o4 | intro, Question, Emails, Rules, Question, Answer | o.md's best layout: question next to the emails *and* rules next to the answer |
| qadj (new) | intro, Rules, Question, Emails, Question, Answer | question next to the emails only (rules stay first): the sliding-window/adjacency mechanism without o4's rules move |
| pad (placebo) | sandwich + a line of 64 periods between the rules and the emails | content-free: shifts and re-encodes every email and answer token about as much as a reorder of the rules block, adds no information |

Not run, and why: a one-line "what to extract" focus line before `Answer:` is c's question-type hint (gold-only +3/−2 over 400 hits, c.md) in another place; the question restated before every email is o7 in all but email 1 (o7 put the question after every email, i.e. before emails 2–5; −0.9 on S300-2). Only one new shape (qadj) is added; it is the clean mechanism test.

**Gold-only harness** (hits only; gold email alone; every arm behind `det(..., { mode: "all" })`): parent `p6-g0` = `a6-det-oracle` = oracles behind det (same prompt byte for byte); arms `p6-g4` (o4), `p6-gqa` (qadj), `p6-gpad` (placebo). Sets: S300-1, S300-4, S300-5, FULL-2, FULL-3 = **1,500 dev hits** (all five hold `i-det-gates`, so the end-to-end stage uses the same questions).

**End to end**: gates (imported, unchanged) behind det, with every sandwich answer prompt (first context and abstention retry) rebuilt in the arm's shape by a ctx proxy (`shapeCtx`; a prompt is rebuilt only if `parseSandwich` from c-agent.js reproduces it byte for byte). Ids `p6-e4`, `p6-eqa`, `p6-epad` (placebo; replaced by `p6-eperm` after S300-1, see §3). Parent: stored `i-det-gates` v2 on the same five sets (2,100 questions).

**Statistics** (`tools/p6-stats.js`): flips (+fixed/−broken) by stratum; question-stratified paired bootstrap and mailbox-cluster paired bootstrap, B = 10,000, `tools/rng.js` mulberry32. Every arm is compared with the det parent and with the placebo arm on the same questions.

**Bar** (BRIEF round 6): Δ ≥ +1.0 hit points vs the det parent pooled over ≥ 900 dev hits, CI lower bound > 0, clearly above the placebo.

**Prior evidence re-scored with p6-stats** (stored answers, not det, so re-roll noise is inside):

| comparison | sets | n | Δ weighted [question-stratified CI] | flips hits | flips misses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| o4 vs gates | S300-1 + S300-2 | 600 | +0.97 [−1.40, +3.33] | +15/−11 | +6/−5 |
| o12 vs gates | S300-1 + S300-2 | 600 | +0.84 [−1.60, +3.26] | +15/−12 | +9/−5 |
| u-gpad (placebo: periods prepended, prompt ×2) vs gates | S300-4 + S300-5 | 600 | +0.66 [−1.40, +2.76] | +12/−9 | +3/−4 |

So before this round o4 was already indistinguishable from a placebo draw on the same scale. (Side check: the stored `i-det-gates` and non-det `gates` texts are identical on all 600 S300-4/5 questions.)

**How much room the window mechanism has** (`tools/p6-swa.js`, offline; 3.60 characters per token calibrated on 143k det-recorded prompt tokens; gates' first contexts on the five sets, 1,500 hits): the gold email is in the first context for 1,483 hits, first for 1,338 (89%), mean length 532 tokens. Entirely within 512 tokens of the leading question: sandwich 47.8%, qadj 56.9%; mean gold tokens beyond the window 189 vs 162; qadj brings ≥ 25 more gold tokens inside the window on 42% of hits. So the reorder acts on the local-layer encoding of the gold email in a large share of hits; if adjacency mattered, it would show.

## 2. Offline check (`tools/p6-check.js S300-1,S300-4`, no GPU)

600 questions, stub generator (abstains on the first call of every 3rd question to exercise the retry):
- gates' first context through the proxy = stored `i-det-gates` contextPaths on 600/600;
- 2,400 answer prompts (incl. 200 retries) rebuilt, 0 left unparsed;
- o4 and qadj prompts are pure reorders (same length, same multiset of lines) on 1,600/1,600; pad = sandwich + the filler line on 800/800;
- `p6-g0`'s gold-only prompt = `sandwichPrompt(question, [gold])` (oracles') on 400/400 hits.

## 3. Gold-only results

**Design change after the first set (S300-1, 20:50 ET), before any other set ran:** the period placebo re-rolled far fewer answers than the reorders (texts changed: pad 83/200, qadj 113/200, o4 134/200), so it under-measures the noise of a reorder. Added **placebo 2, `p6-gperm`**: the sandwich with its first two rule lines swapped (same tokens, same question position, same question-to-email distance; a reorder with no adjacency change). Both placebos are reported. `p6-epad` stays defined; the end-to-end placebo arm is `p6-eperm` (same swap in gates' answer prompts).

Also from 21:30 ET: GPU work goes through `tools/p6-batch.js` (run2.js's loop, copied as in a6-batch.js; several set/variant chunks per lock turn, 14-min budget), because the shared queue held 15–20 jobs and every single-run job re-entered at its back.

S300-1 alone (200 hits, det parent `p6-g0` 91.5): o4 90.5 (+4/−6, 66 identical texts), qadj 93.5 (+5/−1, 87 identical), pad 91.5 (+1/−1, 117 identical). Flips read one by one are the usual judge-level re-wordings (an added source sentence, "the counterparty herself", a second clause). (Check: the non-det `oracles` run on S300-1 has 138/200 texts identical to `p6-g0`, +2/−0.)

**Det reproducibility across workers:** the det gold-only parent exists from three independent runs (`p6-g0` on S300-1; `a6-det-oracle` and q8's Q4 `q8-det-oracle` on S300-4/5, FULL-2/3). Where two exist their texts agree on **1,300/1,300** hits (0 differ). So every flip below is a prompt effect, not run noise.

Every arm, placebos included, loses on FULL-3 (the parent read FULL-3 well; any re-roll regresses it), exactly as every arm "won" on S300-2's unlucky baseline in round 5 (u.md §6).

**Mechanism check** (`tools/p6-flips.js`): if question adjacency mattered through the sliding window, qadj's gains would sit in prompts longer than ~550 tokens (where part of the gold email falls outside the leading question's 512-token window in the sandwich but not in qadj). They do not: prompts ≤ 550 tokens +11/−13, > 550 tokens +14/−16 (o4: +10/−11 and +18/−20). By question type nothing stands out (qadj: other +18/−19, number +3/−0, when +1/−3, url-contact +1/−2).

**Gold-only verdict:** no shape is above the placebo. o4 and qadj each re-roll about 60% of gold-only answer texts and flip ~4% of hit verdicts, half each way; a same-token rule swap does the same. Neither is near the +1.0 bar. By the plan, nothing goes to the end-to-end stage on gold-only evidence; o4 is still run end to end (below) because its only prior evidence was end to end (o.md), and that is the claim to settle. qadj end to end was dropped (04:25) to free GPU: its mechanism acts on the gold email's own encoding, which the gold-only harness measures directly (the gold is first in 89% of gates' hit contexts, with the same window exposure).

**How shape-sensitive hits are at all** (`tools/p6-union.js`, the parent + 4 gold-only arms on 1,500 hits): right under all five prompts 1,307 (87.1%), wrong under all five 82 (5.5%), split 111 (7.4%; the parent is right on 63 of them). A best-of-five oracle would read 94.5 vs 91.3, but no single shape captures it: the flip sets overlap as much with a placebo's as with each other (o4 ∩ rule swap 27 of 59/47 flips; o4 ∩ qadj 22 of 59/54). The flips are a lottery over one fragile set of hits, not a shape effect.

## 5. Conclusion

**The prompt-shape question is closed, negatively.** Behind det (every concordant answer byte-identical, every flip a real prompt effect), at 1,500 gold-only dev hits and 2,100 end-to-end dev questions:
- o4 (question, emails, rules, question), the only later layout that looked positive on two screening sets, is **−0.20 hit points gold-only** (+28/−31) and **−0.17 weighted end to end** (hits +48/−50, misses +9/−13). Against a same-token placebo (two rule lines swapped) it is +0.13 gold-only (+27/−25) and −0.11 end to end (hits +46/−47).
- qadj (rules first, question next to the emails: the sliding-window adjacency mechanism, isolated) is **−0.27 gold-only** (+25/−29), +0.07 vs the placebo (+30/−29), with no gradient by prompt length, i.e. the mechanism predicts nothing.
- Both placebos (a line of periods; a rule-line swap) are −0.27 / −0.33 gold-only and −0.06 end to end: content-free prompt changes flip ~2–4% of gold-only hit verdicts and ~4.5% end to end, symmetrically.
- Bar (Δ ≥ +1.0 hit points vs the det parent over ≥ 900 dev hits, lower bound > 0, above the placebo): **nothing comes close**; every 95% interval is centred on zero and excludes +1.0.

What remains true: the sandwich's original gain (+3.2; gold-only 88.6 → 91.5) was the move from no leading question to one. Once the question precedes the emails, where the rules sit and how close the question is to the emails do not matter for e2b; any further reorder is a re-roll of the same ~7–9% fragile hits, the "prompt lottery" seen in c.md (split hits 13% across renders), b.md (demos), u.md (repetition, padding) and n.md (union of prompts). The S300-1/S300-2 screening wins of o4 and o12 were that lottery on 300-question sets.

**Candidates for H6-C: none.** No p6 variant passed the development bar; no H6-D runs were made.

## 6. Cost and process notes

- GPU: ≈ 30 min of gold-only generation (6,000 hit answers at 220–260 ms, plus det resets) and ≈ 55 min end to end (4,200 answers at ~770 ms). J1 grading ≈ $0.05 (about 2,900 calls; most texts were cached from identical earlier answers).
- The shared GPU queue held 15–20 jobs from 21:00 to 05:00 ET (lead priority runs on H6-C/H6-D, n6, a6, s6, q8, v6). Single-run jobs re-entered at the back of the queue each time, so from 21:30 p6 used `tools/p6-batch.js` (several chunks per lock turn, 14-min budget) with at most two tickets queued. Lesson for a future round: a shared batch runner with a per-turn budget should be the default; per-run jobs cost hours of queue latency per 2 minutes of GPU.
- A driver bug (`grep "0 pending answers"` also matched "11900 pending answers") stopped each driver after one turn; fixed (`"] 0 pending answers"`) and restarted, no work lost or duplicated (pending is recomputed inside the lock).
