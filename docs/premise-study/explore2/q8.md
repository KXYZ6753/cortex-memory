# Worker q8 (round 6): precision diagnostic (Q8_0 vs Q4_0 e2b)

Started Wed 7 Oct ~19:50 ET. A diagnostic, not a candidate.

**Question.** e2b's hit reading has a floor. Gold-only reading reaches 92.2 on hits (FULL-0, `oracles`), and every system reads hits at 88.5–90.5. No prompt, decoding, selection or verification idea has moved it. Is part of that floor the 4-bit quantization? `gemma4:e2b-it-q8_0` (Q8_0, near-lossless) is compared with the study's `gemma4:e2b-it-qat` (Q4_0, quantization-aware trained).

## Setup

- **Variants** (`explore2/variants/q8-precision.js`), both model-agnostic:
  - `q8-det-oracle`: the gold email alone with the sandwich prompt (= `oracles` = `a6-det-oracle` = `p6-g0`), behind `det(all)`, hits only;
  - `q8-det-gates`: `gates`, unchanged, behind `det(all)` (= `i-det-gates@2`).
- **Runner** (`explore2/tools/q8-run.js`): a copy of `run2.js` `runExplore2`'s loop (same ctx, same generation options, same answer record and key). run2.js is not edited. `--model` picks the model:
  - `q4`: `gemma4:e2b-it-qat`, alias `small`, digest checked against the main run's provenance (`explore/run.js` `preflight`), as run2.js does;
  - `q8`: `gemma4:e2b-it-q8_0`, alias label `small-q8`. Its own digest is read from `/api/tags` and pinned in `.data/premise2/explore/q8-pin.json` on first use, and later runs must match the pin. The runner also requires the main run's Ollama build (0.34.2), quantization Q8_0, family gemma4, and the same TEMPLATE / RENDERER / PARSER / PARAMETER lines as the Q4 model, so both models see identically rendered prompts.
- **Store safety:** `answerKey` = sha256(digest | variant@version | questionKey), so Q8 answers can never collide with Q4 answers. Every record also carries `alias: "small-q8"` and `tag`. The runner only runs `q8-*` variant ids, so no Q8 answer appears under another worker's id.
- **GPU discipline:** each chunk is its own `withLock` job of at most 12 minutes. Inside the lock the runner:
  - unloads whatever is resident and loads the tag, as run2.js does;
  - logs `/api/ps` (size vs size_vram, i.e. CPU offload);
  - for Q8, unloads the Q8 model at the end, since it is only this worker's model.

  Chunk summaries go to `.data/premise2/explore/q8-runs.jsonl`.
- **Grading:** `cli2 grade <set> q8-det-oracle`. `gradeExplore` grades every latest final answer of the named variant on the set whatever its alias. It does not filter by alias, so Q8 answers are covered without a wrapper. Verdict keys are model-free (question, references, answer text, judge). `analyzeSet` (`cli2 report`) does filter on alias `small`, so Q8 answers stay out of everyone's standard reports. The Q8 tables come from `tools/q8-table.js`.
- **Analysis** (`explore2/tools/q8-table.js`): arms are (variant@version, alias), J1. Paired Δ (Q8 − Q4) uses questions graded in both arms, with:
  - a question-stratified paired bootstrap and a mailbox-cluster bootstrap, B = 10,000, `tools/rng.js` mulberry32;
  - flips (+fixed / −broken) with an exact sign test;
  - the share of byte-identical answer texts.

## The two models (Ollama `/api/show`, pinned in `q8-pin.json`)

| | Q4 (study) | Q8 |
|---|---|---|
| tag | `gemma4:e2b-it-qat` | `gemma4:e2b-it-q8_0` |
| digest | `07ea59a4…` (= main run) | `95e5aad2…` (pulled Wed 19:54 ET) |
| quantization | Q4_0 (file_type 2) | Q8_0 (file_type 7) |
| file size | 4.34 GB (+ projector blob) | 8.14 GB (one blob) |
| parameter_count (GGUF) | 4.63 B (text model; vision/audio projector in a second blob) | 5.12 B (vision/audio encoders in the same file) |
| GGUF name | `2B_dequant_qat_it_hf` | – |
| Modelfile render lines | TEMPLATE `{{ .Prompt }}`, RENDERER gemma4, PARSER gemma4, temperature 1, top_k 64, top_p 0.95 | identical |

**Caveat on "same weights".** The Q4 model is the **QAT** checkpoint: Google fine-tuned it to be quantized to Q4_0, and its GGUF name is `2B_dequant_qat_it_hf`. The Q8_0 tag is presumably, as with Gemma 3's `-it-q8_0` tags, a post-training quantization of the original (non-QAT) instruct weights; its GGUF carries no name field to confirm it. So the contrast is "the deployed 4-bit QAT model vs a near-lossless 8-bit copy of the original weights". That is the deployment-relevant question: would spending 2× the memory on precision buy reading accuracy? It is not a pure bit-width ablation of one checkpoint. Both models render prompts identically, and the study's options (temperature 0, top_p 1, seed 42, num_ctx 16384, num_batch 512, repeat_penalty 1) override the Modelfile defaults the same way for both. top_k 64 is moot at temperature 0.

**Breakdown** (`tools/q8-flips.js`):
- **2×2 over 1,300 hits:**
  - both right: 1,153;
  - Q8 only: 25;
  - Q4 only: 34;
  - **both wrong: 88 (6.8%)**.
- **Shared floor:** of Q4's 113 wrong hits, Q8 fixes 25 (22%). The union of the two precisions is right on 93.2%. Errors are largely shared, the same pattern as across prompts (n.md, w.md).
- **Text changes vs verdict changes:** Q8 changes **76% of answer texts** (993 / 1,300) but only **4.5% of verdicts** (59), net −9. Identical texts never changed verdict (0 of 307).
- **No length dependence**, which would hint at a precision effect in long multi-email contexts:

  | prompt-token tercile | range | Δ hit | 95% CI |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | short | 344–548 | −0.9 | [−3.0, +1.2] |
  | middle | 548–781 | −0.7 | [−2.5, +1.2] |
  | long | 782–4,682 | −0.5 | [−2.5, +1.6] |

- **No answer-length dependence either:** by Q4 output-token tercile, Δ is −0.7, −1.6 and +0.2.
- **What the flips look like:** both directions are the familiar borderline re-rolls of reading.
  - Q8 adds or drops one item of a list (sitara numbers, "Commitment Section 1" vs "Commitment Section").
  - It picks another phrasing of the same fact ("EBS" vs "Enron Broadband Services").
  - It adds or omits a date.
  - It copies an attachment name in full or in part.

  Nothing suggests a systematic reading gain.
- **Energy** (`tools/q8-energy.js`, s6's session energy logger, gross GPU board energy in the answer windows, no idle subtraction): Q8 uses 51.2 J/q at 107 W vs Q4's 38.0 J/q at 100 W. GPU memory in use was 5.4 GB vs 4.5 GB.

## Steps 3–4: decisions

- **Step 3 (end to end) is not run.** Its trigger was gold-only Δ ≥ +1.0 with a lower bound > 0. The observed Δ is −0.7, and the upper bounds (+0.5 q-strat, +0.6 mailbox) exclude +1.0. The tercile check gives no sign that precision matters more on long inputs, so a Q8 gates run (~1,800 questions, ~30 GPU minutes) has no hypothesis left to test.
- **bf16: not worth it.** Q8_0 is the near-lossless point of the precision ladder: its deviation from bf16 is a small fraction of Q4's. Going from Q4 QAT to Q8_0 already moved verdicts net −0.7 [−1.9, +0.5]. The remaining Q8 → bf16 step is far smaller, so it cannot plausibly produce the ≥ +1.5 needed to matter. It would also cost ~10 GB of disk on a disk with 50 GB free.

## Conclusion

**The e2b hit-reading floor is the model, not the 4-bit quantization.**
- Reading the gold email alone, the near-lossless Q8_0 e2b scores 90.6 vs the deployed Q4_0 QAT e2b's 91.3 on 1,300 development hits: Δ −0.7 [−1.9, +0.5], det vs det, flips +25 / −34. Precision re-rolls about 4.5% of verdicts in both directions, like a prompt perturbation (u's placebo scale, 0.7), and leaves a shared floor of 6.8% of hits that both precisions get wrong.
- For deployment, Q8 costs +26% wall time, +35% GPU energy and +1 GB of VRAM for no accuracy. The QAT Q4_0 model is the right choice, so the study's model choice stands.
- For the paper, this closes the open question: "every system reads hits at 88.5–90.5 vs 92.2 gold-only" is a property of e2b's weights. Higher precision of the same model family does not lift it, and that leaves scale (s6's e4b result) as the lever.
- Caveat: Q4 is the QAT checkpoint and Q8_0 the original instruct weights, so this compares the deployed model with a near-lossless copy of the original, not one checkpoint at two bit widths.
